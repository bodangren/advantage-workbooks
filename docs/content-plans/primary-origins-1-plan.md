## 1. Summary

Origins 1 is the first Primary Advantage book: level 1 (A0-, `cefr_level = 'A0-'`, `ra_level = 1`). It is a story book in the Pip world, like Origins 2 and 3.1, with the easiest language of the series: 14 short stories about the children's first everyday words (greetings, family, colors, numbers to ten, the body, food, clothes, the home, toys, play).

**Not in Origins 1 (Daniel, 2026-10-02):** the alphabet, phonics, and the first 100 words. Thai schools teach the alphabet in kindergarten to grade 2, and the planned Storytime Advantage package will hold alphabet, phonics, and first-words work. This replaces the Origins 3.2 plan line "Letter and sound skills go to Origins 1". The GSE letter and sound objectives (R10.1, R10.3, R14.2, R18.1, R21.3, L10.1, L10.3, L10.5, L16.3) are out of scope for Primary Advantage.

The lesson shape is the same as every Primary book: 12 glossed words, 3 article paragraphs, 10/5/5 app questions, 4 printed MCQ and 1 short answer, the workbook activities, Thai, and 3 pictures. Origins 1 comes before the printed Origins 2, so Tom is 9, Lily is 7, and Pip is a puppy (series bible §2). The QR codes print `o1/<lesson>` (plan D1). The 14 lessons take the 14 oldest level-1 online article ids, in order (track spec, decision 1; ids in `level-plans/bank-1.md`).

## 2. Text profile (`origins-1`)

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Words | 80–120, in 3 paragraphs |
| Mean sentence length | 3.0–4.2 words; longest sentence 7 words or less |
| Running words on Starters (names, glossed, allowed words not counted) | 95% or more |
| Glossed words | 12: 11 or more from Starters, 1 Movers word at most |
| New Starters words | 4 or more per lesson (a target, not a gate) |
| Recycled words | 2 or more glossed words of earlier Origins 1 lessons (from lesson 2) |
| Question marks | 1 or more (book rule: in 10 of 14 lessons) |
| Dialogue | in 4 or more lessons |

Grammar at level 1: *This is … It is … I have … I can see … Look! Where is …? Is it …? Yes, it is. No, it isn't. Let's …* Colors and the numbers one to ten. Present simple only.

## 3. Objectives (A0-, GSE 10–13; letter and sound objectives left out)

Level-1 objectives in Origins 1: R10.2 (question mark), R12.1 and R13.2 (numbers to ten as words), R13.1 (the title of a book on a cover), L10.2 (numbers in spoken sentences), L10.4 (answer with a gesture), L10.6 (familiar nouns and adjectives), L11.1 (polite words), L12.1 (yes/no, good/bad), L13.1 (classroom instructions), L13.2 (greetings and goodbyes). Each is a target in two or more lessons. Level-2 and level-3 objectives can be supporting tags (R17.1, R17.2, L15.1, R21.1, R21.2, L18.1).

## 5. Questions and tasks

- MCQ (4 printed, from the bank of 10): at least 2 test a target objective (numbers: "How many ducks are in the water?"; colors: "What color is the ball?"; polite words: "What does Lily say to Mom?").
- Short answer (1, printed): a personal question in the lesson frame ("What color is your bag?").
- Sentence starters and completion: the lesson frame ("I have a …", "I can see …").
- Writing (level 1): copy and complete 2–3 frames ("My name is ___.", "I like ___.").
- Vocabulary fill: 4 short sentences from the text with `___`.
