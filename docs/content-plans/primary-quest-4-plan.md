## 1. Summary

Quest 4 is the first A1 book: level 4 (A1-, `cefr_level = 'A1-'`, `ra_level = 4`). It goes to the printer with Origins 3.2 and the Origins 3.1 insert by 2026-10-24. The four Quest books share the 86 A1 young-learner reading and listening objectives (`data/a1-objective-key.json`). Quest 4 takes the lower part (GSE 22–25) and makes the step from the Starters list to the Movers list. Genre mix: 8 stories and 6 functional texts. The lesson shape does not change. QR key: `q4/<lesson>`.

**Cast (decision, track spec §5):** the Pip family continues, one school year older. Tom is 10, Lily is 8, May is 9 (her ninth birthday is not shown), Pat is 6, Mia and Leo are 8, Ben is 10, Sam is 9. Looks, homes, and places stay as in the series bible, so the cast sheets stay the same. Pip is still a small brown puppy with a red collar.

## 2. Text profile (`quest-4`)

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Words | 190–250, in 3 paragraphs |
| Mean sentence length | 5.6–7.0 words; longest sentence 12 words or less |
| Running words on Starters or Movers (names, glossed, allowed words not counted) | 95% or more |
| Glossed words | 12: 7 or more from Movers, 1 Flyers word at most, none above Flyers |
| New Movers words | 6 or more per lesson (a target, not a gate) |
| Recycled words | 4 or more glossed words of earlier lessons (Origins and Quest) |
| Question marks | 2 or more (book rule: in 10 of 14 lessons) |
| Dialogue | in 6 or more lessons |

Grammar at level 4 (Movers grammar): contractions (*I'm, he's, we're, it's, don't, can't, isn't*); present continuous (*Tom is riding his bike.*); *can / can't* for ability; *like / don't like + -ing*; *Do you …? Does she …?*; ordinal numbers; times (*a quarter past seven, half past eight, ten to nine*); *there is / there are* with *some / any*; comparatives with *-er* (one or two); *must* for rules. Past simple only in fixed phrases (one or two "was"); the stories stay in the present.

Days of the week and months: put them in `allow` (Movers and Flyers words in the running text).

## 4. Questions and tasks

- MCQ (4 printed of 10): at least 2 test a target objective (timetable: "What time is English on Monday?"; can/can't: "What can't Tom do?").
- Short answer (1): a personal question in the lesson frame ("What do you like doing at the weekend?").
- Writing: a personal version of the text type (L01: "Write about you." L09: "Write about your street." L10: "Write the weather for today.").
