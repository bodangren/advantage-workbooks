# Cover art plan

Owner: Daniel Bo. Daniel wrote the table about two years ago and confirmed it on 2026-10-07 ("I think
it still works"). Track: `measure/tracks/book_covers_20261007/`.

## Rules (2026-10-07)

- **One scene for each book.** The back art and the front art are one continuous picture. In the
  flat cover, the back is on the left and the front is on the right. New books get one wide
  picture that the cover script cuts into the back and the front.
- **Model and size.** Muse (`meta/muse-image` through OpenRouter) with three reference pictures: the
  heroes (cut from the Quest 4 front), the fox (cut from the Origins 3.2 front), and a cover art for
  the style. mmx image-01 failed the test (other style, other faces). Muse gives 1920 × 1280 px for
  `3:2`; the cut gives two pages of 943 × 1280 px (about 114 ppi at 210 × 285 mm; the Canva art of
  levels 1–4 is about 128 ppi).
- **Prompt.** Say "one single continuous wide painting, no divider" and never "left half = back
  cover" (Muse then draws a line in the middle). Place the heroes at about three quarters of the
  width, heads at the middle of the height, feet one sixth above the bottom.
- **Layout zones.** The front has the level bar and the PA logo at the top and the title banner at
  the bottom; the heroes stand in the middle of the front half, away from the center line. The back
  has the logos and the CEFR badge at the top, the paper panel with the text in the middle, and the
  wooden sign and the QR code at the bottom; the back half needs scenery only, no heroes.
- **Cast.** The same two heroes (the boy in green with the sword, the girl in red with the crystal
  staff) and the fox companion on every book. Their gear grows with the level, as the table says.

## Concepts and progression

| Level | Cover Concept | Character / Ability Progression |
|---|---|---|
| **Origins 1** | Tutorial village path | Simple clothes, wooden practice sword, small satchel, first map; “starting the journey.” |
| **Origins 2** | Forest edge / first discovery | Slightly better boots, cloaklets, lantern, compass; cautious but excited. |
| **Origins 3.1** | Training meadow / old signpost | Beginner gear plus first badge or charm; small magical glow, no danger. |
| **Origins 3.2** | Hidden garden / puzzle gate | Same level as 3.1, but puzzle-focused; book, key, rune stones, teamwork pose. |
| **Quest 4** | Castle road / bright fantasy valley | First “real” adventurer outfits; sword and crystal staff, as in your sample. |
| **Quest 5** | River crossing / bridge challenge | Upgraded belts, gloves, small shields, map case; teamwork movement. |
| **Quest 6.1** | Ancient ruins entrance | Same Quest-tier gear but with relics, torches, stone tablets. |
| **Quest 6.2** | Forest shrine / creature encounter | Same level as 6.1, but nature-magic variation; friendly creature or guardian. |
| **Adventure 7.1** | Mountain pass | Stronger boots, capes, climbing tools; more confident posture. |
| **Adventure 7.2** | Sky bridge / canyon crossing | Same ability tier; wind, ropes, gliders, teamwork balance. |
| **Adventure 8.1** | Crystal cave | More magical effects; staff crystal grows, sword has a faint glow. |
| **Adventure 8.2** | Waterfall temple | Same level variation; water runes, blue lighting, puzzle door. |
| **Adventure 8.3** | Library ruins | Same level variation; reading magic, floating pages, ancient language motif. |
| **Adventure 9.1** | Desert or sunlit ruins | More advanced explorer gear; scarves, goggles, artifact compass. |
| **Adventure 9.2** | Jungle temple | Same tier; vines, animal companion, hidden doorway. |
| **Adventure 9.3** | Night camp / monster shadow | Same tier but more suspense; strategic readiness, not scary violence. |
| **Hero 10.1** | Fortress approach | Armor pieces appear: bracers, shoulder guards, heroic capes. |
| **Hero 10.2** | Gate defense / shield moment | Same level variation; protective magic, teamwork stance. |
| **Hero 11.1** | Dragon valley, distant dragon | More commanding posture; glowing weapon and stronger staff. |
| **Hero 11.2** | Fire-lit cavern | Same tier; flame runes, protective spell, courage under pressure. |
| **Hero 11.3** | Ice peak / frozen gate | Same tier; elemental variation, ice crystal, warm cloaks. |
| **Hero 12.1** | Ancient city / leadership moment | They look like young champions; banners, allies in distance, no adult look. |
| **Hero 12.2** | Airship dock / sky city | Same level variation; travel gear, sky map, mechanical-magic elements. |
| **Hero 12.3** | Giant library / knowledge trial | Same level variation; reading becomes magic, glowing books, wisdom theme. |
| **Legend 13.1** | Floating island kingdom | Legendary capes, refined armor, radiant artifacts. |
| **Legend 13.2** | Star bridge / constellation gate | Same tier; cosmic navigation, star compass, calm confidence. |
| **Legend 14.1** | Mythic castle in clouds | Near-final hero look; polished gear, strong magical aura. |
| **Legend 14.2** | Celestial forest / guardian beast | Same tier; bond with majestic creature, harmony not combat. |
| **Legend 14.3** | Ancient throne hall / trial of courage | Same tier; ceremonial, heroic, high-stakes but safe. |
| **Legend 15.1** | Final ascent / golden summit | Full fantasy hero level; legendary sword/staff, epic light. |
| **Legend 15.2** | Star temple / wisdom mastery | Same final tier; knowledge, books, constellations, glowing portal. |
| **Legend 15.3** | World tree / completed journey | Same final tier; triumphant, peaceful, mastery achieved. |
