## 2. The most frequent faults

The script checks did not find these faults.

- **Reported speech.** Level 7 does not allow it, but the drafts used "said that" and "asked if" in texts, questions, and model answers (12 articles in b001–b036 and 13 in b037–b072). The fix is a direct quote.
- **An MCQ option that is also true, or a false marked answer.** For example: b004 m6 (two true places); b014 m6 (the marked answer was not the first thing that Coach Matt said); b011 m7 (Mia writes to the group, not to Lily); b020 m9 and b002 m7 (an option close to true).
- **Story logic.** Badges made and sold in one club hour (b007); a bear that promises never to laugh again, but never laughed (b006); "the three jobs" with two jobs in the text (b027); a bird in a game whose rule says "sea animal" (b043); a postcard out of time order (b054); Tom walks to school, but is late because the bus was slow (b019); a man who is already inside is told "Please come in" (b028).

- **Picture filter words and distress poses.** "chin on her hand", "head on a table", "arms around his knees", "funny ghost pose" (b008, b015, b017, b019).
- **The calendar.** Three articles used Saturdays in November (b050, b059, b060). Level 7 is the first term (June to October 2026), and Adventure 8.1 has the aquarium trip on Saturday, November 14, and Green Hill on Saturday, November 21. The editor moved them to free Saturdays of the first term: September 12, September 19, and October 3.
