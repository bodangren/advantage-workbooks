## 2. The most frequent faults

The script checks did not find these faults.

- **"said / told / thought that" + a present-tense clause in a past-tense story** (in most lessons of all three books). Level 9 allows say / tell + that only with no tense change. The fix is a direct quote.
- **Questions in the present tense for a past-tense story.** Questions about a sign, a program, or an email in the present stay in the present.
- **Fill items with two answers** (because/since, so/and, bake/cook) and MCQ options that were also true.
- **A blog post written as if it was not yet online** (9.1 L07, L10; 9.2 L10, L13; 9.3 L05).
- **Story logic.** Points for answers that nobody gave (9.2 L02); medicine at six o'clock with "one spoon at bedtime" on the label (9.2 L05); children who ran across a crossing for a train (9.2 L09); lunch at noon after a course that ends at eleven (9.3 L04); Ravi as Lily's pen pal (he is Leo's; 9.3 L03).
- **A false fact about the real world.** 9.3 L11 said that an invented engineer built the first tram of Thailand in 1905. It is now the first tram of the story's own town, with no country.
- **Pictures** that did not show their own paragraph (9.1 L01, L08; 9.2 L07, L13).
- **Thai.** Clock times (9.2 L03: เก้าโมงเย็น for nine p.m.; 9.3 L10: 2:20 for twenty to three); "นาย" from a girl to a boy (9.2 L01, L02); "สวัสดีตอนบาย" for "good afternoon" (9.2 L11; Claude fixed it there and in 8.2 L03, and two older books follow after the load).
