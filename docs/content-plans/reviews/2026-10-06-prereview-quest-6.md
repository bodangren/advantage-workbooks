## 2. The most frequent faults

- **An MCQ with two true options, or an answer that the text does not give** (6.1 L07, L08, L13; 6.2 L12 m8).
- **A fill item with more than one possible answer** (about 5 in 6.1 and 12 in 6.2).
- **Story logic.** A phone number written before Coach Matt repeats it (6.1 L04); a helmet in two places (6.2 L05); shoes in the kitchen while Lily is in the living room (6.1 L10); a "last line" that was not the last (6.1 L05).
- **A picture plan that does not agree with the text.** A morning scene for an afternoon call (6.1 L04); a teapot before the tea (6.1 L05); a ball in Pip's mouth and in the glove (6.1 L10); one "ribbon" for the finish tape and the prize (6.2 L10).
- **A speaker that is not clear** (6.2 L12, L13, L14), and reported speech in model answers (6.1 L10, L11).
- **Thai.** "in a minute" as "เดี๋ยวนี้" (right now) in 6.1 L04; "of course" as "ชอบสิ" in 6.1 L06; long time phrases now "สิบห้านาที" (6.1 L14).
