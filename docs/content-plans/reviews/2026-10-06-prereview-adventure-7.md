## 2. The most frequent faults

- **A fill item with more than one possible answer** (about 25 in 7.1 and 8 in 7.2).
- **An MCQ option that is also true by the text** (7.1 L03, L06, L07, L10; 7.2 L07, L14).
- **Story logic.** Sign numbers that did not agree (7.1 L03); a market trip six days before the fresh fruit was needed (7.1 L12); two "first posts" about one trip (7.1 L07, L08); a fire engine at half past ten after an order to "wait here" at eight (7.2 L11); a theater on the map with no symbol in the key (7.2 L14); a twenty-baht coin (7.2 L09).
- **A picture plan that does not agree with the text** (7.1 L03, L05, L09, L11, L13; 7.2 L08, L10, L11, L12).
- **Thai.** "นาย" from a girl to a boy (7.1 L12); "ตะเกียง" (oil lamp) for a lamp (7.2 L03).
