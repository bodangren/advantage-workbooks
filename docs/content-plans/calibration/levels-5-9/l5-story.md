On Saturday, Tom and Lily found an old map in Grandpa's book. The map showed a forest, a river, and a waterfall. A red X was behind the waterfall. "It's a treasure map!" said Lily. "Maybe a pirate drew it!" "Let's go," said Tom. They put some sandwiches in a bag and called Pip. Pip ran to the door first, and his tail went round and round.

The forest was quiet and cold. They looked at the map again and again. Suddenly, they heard a terrible noise. Lily was afraid, and she took Tom's hand. "Don't be afraid," said Tom. "I'll go first." But Pip ran in front of them. He liked to hide behind the trees. "Pip, where are you?" shouted Lily. "Come back!"

Then the children came to a river. There was a small bridge on it. "It looks dangerous," said Lily. "The bridge is old." "We have to be brave," said Tom. They walked on the bridge slowly, one by one. They did not look down. Pip was on the bridge first, and he was not afraid at all. The noise was louder now, and it came from the waterfall!

The red X was on a big rock behind the water. Pip dug under the rock, and he found an old box. "Is it a pirate's box?" asked Tom. Inside, there was a book and a letter. The letter said, "My treasure. Jim, age eleven." "That's Grandpa's name!" said Lily. At home, Grandpa laughed. "I hid that box when I was a boy," he said. "Thank you, Pip! Now it's your treasure."
