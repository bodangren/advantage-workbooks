Tom and Lily want to make a fruit salad for the school picnic. Mom is going to help them. You can make a fruit salad, too. You need some strawberries, two bananas, an apple, and an orange. You also need a bowl, a knife, and a big spoon. For the sauce, you need some yoghurt and a little honey. It is easy, and it takes about twenty minutes. Are you ready? Let's start! Tom likes strawberries best.

First, wash your hands. Then wash the fruit in cold water. Ask Mom to help with the knife. It is not safe for small hands. Next, cut the apple and the bananas into small pieces. Cut the strawberries in half. Put all the fruit into the bowl. Take the orange and cut it into pieces, too. Don't put the seeds in the bowl. They taste bad. Put them in the bin.

Now make the sauce in a cup. Mix the yoghurt and the honey with a spoon. If you like sweet food, you can add a little sugar. Put the sauce on the fruit, and mix everything slowly. Lily thinks that it looks wonderful. Tom can't wait. He is hungry! Mom says that they should put the bowl in the fridge. It must stay there for one hour.

Finally, the fruit salad is ready. Tom and Lily put it on the table with some small plates. Everyone at the picnic says that it is delicious. Pip sits under the table and waits for a piece of banana. Mom says, "Now we should tidy the kitchen!" Would you like to make a fruit salad? What fruit will you choose?
