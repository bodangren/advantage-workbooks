On Saturday afternoon, Tom came home from the football match. He did not say hello to anyone. He walked past the kitchen, went straight to the garden, and sat under the old mango tree. Pip followed him and put his head on Tom's knee. Lily watched them from the window. She knew that something was wrong, because Tom always talked about his games.

Later, Lily took two glasses of juice to the garden. "Do you want to tell me about the match?" she asked. "Are you upset?" "No, I'm fine," said Tom. "There is nothing to tell. We lost. It's no big deal." However, Lily noticed that his juice was still full an hour afterwards. Tom always finished his juice. She also noticed that his football bag was lying in the grass. He did not look at it once.

That evening, Grandpa came for dinner. Mom had cooked his favorite soup, but Tom did not eat much. Dad said that the team had played well. Tom looked at his plate and said nothing. Grandpa said that he had a story from his own school days. "I once missed an easy goal in an important match," he said. "I did not play football anymore for a month. I thought that everybody was laughing at me."

"What happened?" asked Lily. "My coach gave me some good advice," said Grandpa. "He said that every player makes mistakes, and that the best players continue to play. A mistake is not the end of the game. It is only a part of it." Tom listened carefully, but he did not look up. Grandpa smiled, and then he began to talk about the weather. He certainly knew when to stop.

After dinner, Tom went out to the garden again. This time, he took his football with him. Lily watched from the door. Tom kicked the ball against the wall, again and again. Pip ran after it, and the old memory of the match seemed to disappear. Soon, Tom looked up and saw his sister. "Do you want to play?" he asked. "I can offer you a good price," said Lily. "I'll be the goalkeeper, and you will have to score ten goals."

They played until it was dark. Afterwards, they sat on the grass and shared an orange. "Thank you," said Tom quietly. "For what?" asked Lily. "For the juice," he said. She did not say anything, but she smiled. Next Saturday, there was a new match, and a small sign on the garden wall: "Tom's Goal Practice. Receive one free orange for each goal."
