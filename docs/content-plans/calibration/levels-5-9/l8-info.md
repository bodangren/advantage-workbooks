Subject: Our Food Festival.
Dear Anna and all our friends.
Thank you for your email and for the photos of your school. We have been members of the Adventure Club for two years. We love your photos of the town. Now we want to tell you about our big event. We are very excited, and we have been busy for weeks. It is the school food festival, and it is this Saturday.

The festival is an international event. Each class cooks a dish from a different country. Our class is making a Thai dish. We are cooking sticky rice with mango, because it is popular with children everywhere. The recipe is simple. You need rice, coconut milk, sugar, and fresh mango. We buy the ingredients at the local market. Teacher Kim gives us a discount, so the food is not expensive. Last year, we sold two hundred plates. Everyone said that the rice was the best food of the day.

Parents and friends are coming, too. It will be a happy day. The festival includes music, games, and a quiz. Tickets are available at the school gate. They are only twenty baht, so everybody can come. At two o'clock, Tom is meeting the judges, and Lily is giving a short talk about our recipe. Later, we are going to relax in the garden with some cold juice and watch the dancers.

We would like to send you a picture of our dish. Would you please send us a picture of yours? Please tell us about the food in your country, and send us your favorite recipe. Is there a festival in your town? Do you eat special food? We think that your festival will be fantastic. What are you cooking this year?

We are waiting for your answer. Please write soon. If you write soon, we will put your picture on our class blog. You don't have to write a long email. A few words and a picture are enough for us. We will be very happy to read them. Our class is also going to make a video of the day. We will send you the link.
Best wishes from all of us, Lily and Tom.
