Lily has been a member of the Photo Club since September. Today there is a big competition at school. The prize is a new camera. The hall is crowded with parents and children, and Lily feels nervous. "If I win, I will take photos of everything," she tells Mia. "And if you don't win?" asks Mia. "Then I will be sad for a few days," says Lily. "But I am not going to cry."

The teachers look at the photos on the wall. Teacher Kim has a serious face. She says nothing for a long time. The room is very quiet, and Lily can hear her own heart. Lily has taken more than a hundred photos this year. She has chosen only one for the competition. It shows Pip asleep under a tree. His nose is in the grass, and one ear is up. Lily thinks that it is a perfect photo, because it makes everyone smile when they see it.

Then Lily sees another photo. It is similar to hers. It also shows a sleeping dog under a tree! "Whose photo is that?" she asks, and she speaks very quietly. "It's mine," says Ben. "That is my dog, Max." Lily feels sick. "Our photos are too similar," she thinks. "The teachers will not like both of them."

While Lily is waiting, Tom comes to her. He has seen both photos. "In my opinion, they are different," he says. "Your photo is warm, and Ben's photo is funny. You don't have to worry." Lily smiles. She does not feel nervous now. She is going to enjoy the afternoon. She is especially happy that her brother is here.

Teacher Kim takes the microphone. "Two photos are exactly the same size, and both are very good," she says. "This year, we have two winners!" Lily and Ben jump up. They shake hands, and everybody in the hall claps for a long time. The two friends celebrate with juice and cake. Later, Pip and Max meet in the garden. They run around together, because they do not know that they are famous. Both dogs are too tired to walk home, so Tom has to carry them.
