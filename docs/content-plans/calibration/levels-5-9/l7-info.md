Our Adventure Club has just visited the nature park near our school. We have written about it on the class blog. This post tells you about the wild animals that we saw. There is a map at the end. Use its key to find each animal.

Tortoises live in the quiet corner of the park, near the lake. A tortoise has a hard shell on its back. The shell is made of bone. Tortoises walk very slowly, but they can live for a hundred years. Mia has never seen such an old creature before. We gave one tortoise a piece of apple. It ate the apple in a minute. Then it looked at us and asked for more! At least, that is what Lily thinks.

The park is also full of insects. There are several kinds of butterflies in the flower garden. A butterfly has four wings, and each wing has beautiful colors. Some butterflies fly for hundreds of kilometers! We saw a lot of small black bugs on the ground, too. Teacher Kim says that there are millions of them in the park. We think that the park is a good home for them, because nobody picks the flowers.

Birds have built their nests in the tall trees by the river. We could not see the nests, because they were very high. But we heard the baby birds. They sounded like little whistles! Near the forest, Tom saw a small deer. It has soft brown fur and big, round ears. It looked at us for a second. Then it ran away.

Have you ever been to a nature park? What have you seen there? The park is open every day. The ticket is forty baht. You can stay at the camp and sleep in a tent. Come and start your own adventure! Take a bottle of water, and bring a camera. We hope that you will have a wonderful day. We have already planned our next trip.
