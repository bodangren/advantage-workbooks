This is my family page. My name is Lily, and I am nine. I live with my parents, my brother Tom, and my puppy, Pip. Tom is eleven. He is taller than I am. His hair is curly, but my hair is straight. I am not a grown-up!

My dad is a bus driver. He gets up at five o'clock every morning. He drives a big bus to the city. When he comes home, he is always hungry and thirsty. My mom is a nurse. She works at the hospital, and she helps sick children. My aunt Mia is a doctor. She works at the hospital, too. She is never sad, but she is often tired.

My grandparents live near our house. Grandpa has a white beard and a moustache. He is thin, but he is very strong. Grandma is a cook. She makes the best noodles in the world. Tom is their grandson, and I am their granddaughter. We go to their house every Sunday. We eat rice and fish, and Grandpa tells funny stories.

We are all very different. Tom is quiet, but I am loud. Dad is the tallest in the family, and Grandpa is the oldest. Who is the funniest? Pip is the funniest! He runs after the cat every day, and he never catches it. Who is in your family? Is it a big family or a small family?
