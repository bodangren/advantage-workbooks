The Adventure Club went to camp for the first time. There were twelve children, and Teacher Kim came with them. Lily had never slept in a tent before. "Have you ever been to a camp?" she asked Mia. "No, never," said Mia. "But I have already got my torch." Tom was thirteen and went to the camp as a helper. He had brought Pip, too.

After dinner, the children sat around the fire. It was warm, and the sky was full of stars. Teacher Kim told them a story about a wild creature in the forest. It had big eyes and a long tail. "No one has ever seen it," she said, "but people hear it at night." Mia laughed, but Lily felt a little afraid. Then they all went to bed.

At midnight, Lily woke up. Something was moving outside the tent, and it sounded like a small animal. She wanted to wake Mia, but Mia was asleep. Lily took her torch, but it was broken. She did not look at it before the trip. It was dark, and she was alone. Her heart was beating fast. "Shall I wait until morning?" she thought. "No, I must decide now."

Lily put on her coat and went out very slowly. The noise came from an empty box next to the fire. She came closer and looked inside. Two small eyes looked back at her. It was Pip! He had eaten the last sandwich, and now he could not get out. "You are the creature!" Lily whispered. She laughed so much that she fell down on the grass.

The next morning, the children heard the story. "Pip has just found the best adventure of the camp," said Tom. "Next time, I will hurry to bed before dinner," said Mia. Teacher Kim smiled at the little dog. "Tonight, we are going to explore the forest in the day," she said. "And I hope everyone has a torch!" Pip said, "Woof!"
