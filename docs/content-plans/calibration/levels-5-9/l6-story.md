It was Costume Day at Lily's school. Lily was going to be a queen. She had a gold crown and a long red dress. Tom was going to be a robot, but his costume was a secret. That morning, he put an enormous box on his head. Then he went out of the house. "You will see it at school," he said.

When they got to school, it was raining. Lily opened her umbrella, but Tom did not have one. "Your robot might get wet," said Lily. "Are you sure about the box?" "It will be fine," said Tom. "I'm going to run." He ran across the school garden. The rain fell on his silver paper, and the paper began to disappear. Then the box fell apart. Everyone in the garden stopped and looked at him.

Tom stood in the rain with a small piece of box in his hands. He felt terrible. Lily went to him and whispered, "What's wrong?" Then she said, "Don't worry. I have an idea." She took a plastic bag from her pocket and put it on his head. Then she put her umbrella over both of them. "Now you are a rain robot," she said. "Robots can walk in the rain!"

Tom looked up and laughed, and then everyone laughed with him. Even the teachers smiled at him. Teacher Kim clapped her hands. "What a wonderful idea!" she said. "The prize for the best costume goes to the rain robot." Tom was so happy that he forgot the box. After school, Pip met them at the gate. He was wearing a small paper crown. "Woof!" said Pip. It was the biggest surprise of the day.
