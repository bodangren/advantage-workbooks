Every morning, we turn on the light in our classroom without thinking about it. However, electricity does not come from the wall. It is made in a power station, and then it is carried to our school along long wires. Our Adventure Club wanted to learn more, so we visited a small power station near our town. This is what we found out. It was a normal school day, but we did not want it to end.

A scientist from Italy was one of the first people to study electricity. His name was Alessandro Volta. More than two hundred years ago, he made the first battery from two kinds of metal. It was a simple invention, but it changed the world. Before that time, nobody could keep electricity for later. Today, we use batteries in phones, watches, torches, and cars. Many people discover new ideas every year, and they make better batteries.

Most of our electricity is made from gas, coal, and water. At the power station that we visited, water is used. A big wall, called a dam, holds the water of the river. When the gates are opened, the water falls down a pipe. It turns a large wheel, and the wheel makes electricity. The engineer told us that this kind of power is clean. There is no smoke, and nothing is burned.

Recently, many countries have started to use the sun, too. Solar panels are built on roofs and in fields. They are made of glass and special metal. In Thailand, there are national plans for more solar power. Teacher Kim says that the reason is simple: the sun shines for free. However, solar panels cost a lot of money. They do not work at night.

We did an experiment in class. We made a small battery from a lemon, two coins, and a wire. It gave only a little power, but it was enough for a small light. Lily thought that it would not work. Tom said that it was a normal lemon. She was surprised when the light came on. "That is the nature of science," said Teacher Kim. "You never know until you try."

What can we do at home? First, we can turn off the lights when we leave a room. Second, we can use less hot water. Third, we can ask our parents to buy products that use little power. Probably the most important thing is to talk about it with our friends. A small change in many homes is a big change for the whole country.
