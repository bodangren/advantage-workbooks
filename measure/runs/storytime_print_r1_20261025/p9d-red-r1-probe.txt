The 8 week files carry the record and stay uncommitted. They are flagged `--assume-unchanged` for
the run, so `git status --porcelain content/storytime` prints nothing and the J18 guard of the
loops track sees the content folder as it is after the Green commit of the 8 week files. The flag
is cleared with `git update-index --no-assume-unchanged` and the files are restored with
`git checkout --` after the run.
