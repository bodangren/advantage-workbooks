## The finding of this gate

The record of D-P9D-1 carries a `source` key. The print approval part of `StorytimeWeekSchema`
(`dashboard/lib/storytime/schema.ts`, `StorytimePrintApprovalSchema`) is strict and holds
`status`, `date`, and `words` only. The part rejects the `source` key, so the week files with the
record do not parse, and every case that reads a shipped week fails with the ZodError of that key.

The gate therefore does not pass. The fix is production code: the print approval part must accept
a `source`, like the six app approval parts. The Red role writes no production code.

The F3 scope compare needs no further change: the `print` key of the approval block is now in the
skip list, so no scope problem appears. The F3 scope case fails with the ZodError of the schema,
not with a scope message.
