## The caption rules (spec FR-14 rules 6 and 7, spec D19)

1. A caption has 1 to 40 words. The count trims the text, splits it on runs of white space, and counts the non-empty parts.
2. A caption holds no pipe, no angle bracket, and no line break.
3. A caption does not end in a media file extension, for example `.mp4` or `.jpg`.
4. A caption is for its week. It names the English of the moment: the sound and a word, the frame sentence, the book title, or the song title.
5. A caption names no child.
6. A caption holds no file path and no assessment word.
7. The teacher posts the caption with no change.
8. The 40 captions are all different, and the 5 captions of one week are all different.
