# Origins 2 (A0) — Semester Test Plan (Lessons 1–8)

**Version:** 0.1 (draft for approval)
**Date:** 2026-09-14
**Status:** PLAN — not yet a test paper
**Scope:** End-of-semester test for students who completed Origins 2 Lessons 1–8.

---

## 1. Design Principles

1. **Familiar structure.** Every section mirrors a workbook step students have done 8 times. Same formats: 4-option MC, word-bank fill-in, matching with letters, word-ordering, sentence completion, 2-sentence writing.
2. **A test, not a lesson.** No teacher modeling, no first-listen-together, no self-assessment checkboxes, no AI chatbot step. Teacher reads instructions; students work alone.
3. **Unseen material, practiced language.** Both passages are new stories built ONLY from L1–L8 vocabulary, article words, and the 16 sentence patterns in `sentence-patterns-L1-L8.md`. Machine-validated: 0 new words, 0 new patterns.
4. **Four skills covered.** Reading, Listening, Writing in the written paper. Oral reading via a 1:1 Running Record.
5. **Honest scoring.** 100 points for the paper. Running Record reported separately as a diagnostic (accuracy %, error analysis, fluency note) — recommendation below.

## 2. Test Materials (two passages, both already constrained to L1–L8)

| Material | Story | Reuse |
|----------|-------|-------|
| Reading article | "A Day at the Park" (48 sentences, ~190 words) — reunites Pip, Lily, Ben, Mia, Tom | Already written + validated in `test-article-L1-L8.md` |
| Listening passage | "Pip at the Farm" (~90 words, new mini-story) — recycles L2 farm/garden content in new combinations | To be finalized (validated draft in §6) |
| Running Record text | "Pip and the Ball" (~100 words) — paragraphs 1–2 of the park story | Subset of reading article, administered 1:1 BEFORE students see the paper |

**Why three texts:** the listening passage must differ from the reading passage (else it is a memory test, not a listening test). The Running Record must be read BEFORE the written paper, or oral-reading accuracy is contaminated by silent reading.

## 3. Section Blueprint (mirrors workbook Step → test Part)

Written paper: **60 minutes, 100 points.**

| Test Part | Mirrors Workbook Step | Format | Items | Points |
|-----------|----------------------|--------|-------|--------|
| **A. Words I Know** | Step 2 Key Vocabulary ("Know it?" column) | 12 words from the semester list. Student ticks the words they know AND writes the Thai meaning for 6 chosen words. | 12 + 6 | 12 |
| **B. Listening** | Step 3 First Reading with Audio (listen for overall meaning) | Teacher reads "Pip at the Farm" aloud twice (standard script). Then: 5 MC questions (4 options) + 3 listen-and-judge items (teacher reads a sentence; student circles ✓ or ✗ against a written sentence). | 8 | 16 |
| **C. Reading** | Steps 5+7 In-Depth Reading + Comprehension Check | Students read "A Day at the Park" silently. 6 MC questions (4 options, same a/b/c/d layout) + 2 short-answer questions (Step 8 format, 1 sentence expected). | 8 | 24 |
| **D. Vocabulary & Sentences** | Steps 9+10 Vocabulary Practice + Sentence Practice | D1: match 8 words to definitions (letter format, like vocab_match). D2: fill 5 sentences with word bank (like vocab_fill). D3: order 3 scrambled sentences (like sentence_order). D4: complete 3 sentence prompts (like sentence_completion). | 19 | 28 |
| **E. Writing** | Step 11 Writing Practice | E1 guided: copy and finish 2 sentences with given words. E2 free: "Write about Pip at the park. Write 2 sentences." Scored with rubric (capital letter, end mark, word use, message clear). | 2 tasks | 20 |
| **F. My Test Reflection** | Step 13 Lesson Reflection | Non-scored. 3 smiley-face items ("How do you feel?"). Keeps the ritual, costs 1 minute, zero anxiety added. | — | — |

**Point check:** A 12 + B 16 + C 24 + D 28 + E 20 = **100**.

**Omitted on purpose:** Step 1 (interest rating), Step 4 (collect vocabulary), Step 6 (collect sentences), Step 12 (AI chatbot) — these are learning behaviors, not measurable outcomes.

## 4. Running Record Design (1:1, ~3 minutes per student)

**Administration:** before the written paper (students read the RR text with the teacher one at a time while the rest do a quiet puzzle/coloring page), OR on the previous day. Same week as the paper.

**Text:** first ~100 words of "A Day at the Park" (paragraphs 1–2). Every word and pattern is from L1–L8. The student has NOT seen the paper yet.

**Scoring sheet (per student):**

1. **Accuracy** = (Total words − Errors) ÷ Total words × 100.
   - Errors: substitution, omission, insertion. Repetitions and self-corrections are NOT errors.
   - Teacher codes each error M / S / V (Meaning–Structure–Visual) using standard Reading Record marks (sample codes on the sheet).
2. **Bands (A0, end of semester 1 — adjustable after the pilot):**
   - 95%+ independent on practiced text
   - 90–94% instructional — normal expected range
   - below 90% — needs re-teaching support next term
3. **Fluency note:** 3-point scale (word-by-word / some phrasing / reads in phrases), plus WPM if easy to time.
4. **Not folded into the 100-point score.** It is a diagnostic for grouping next term. Rationale: one 3-minute sample is too small for a grade, and mixing it into the paper score punishes nervous readers twice.

---

## 5. Listening Administration Protocol

- Teacher reads the passage **twice** at normal slow pace (script has pause marks).
- Students keep the paper closed during both readings; questions open only after the second reading. (This matches Step 3: listen first for meaning.)
- The 3 listen-and-judge items are read a third time, one sentence per item, with a 5-second pause each.
- Option later: record a studio audio file for consistency across schools. For 2 schools, a scripted live read is reliable and needs no equipment.

## 6. Listening Passage Draft — "Pip at the Farm" (to be machine-validated)

> Pip is at the farm. The farm is big. A farmer is here. The farmer is nice. This is Anna. Anna is a girl. She is with Pip. Anna sees a garden. The garden is green. Green plants are here. The farmer shows a carrot plant. The carrot is orange. The farmer gives water. The sun is hot. The plants grow big. Pip sees a dog. The dog is brown. The dog is funny. Pip likes the dog. Anna likes the farm. This is a good day.

All words and patterns from L1–L8 corpus (farm content recycles L2; "funny", "brown" recycle L8/L5; "Anna" is a new name introduced with the standard A3+A2 pattern, same as Ben in L5). Final validation runs before the paper is written.
