1 Grandma has a big bag. It is morning. Grandma, Tom, and Pip walk to the market. There are a lot of shops. Pip walks next to Tom. "Look at the fruit shop!" says Tom. "How many mangoes do you want?" asks the woman. "Six mangoes, please," says Grandma. "Here you are." "Thank you!" says Grandma.
2
Six mangoes, please.
Grandma wants bananas, limes, and lemons, too. She wants apples and one big coconut. Tom holds the bag. Now they go to the egg shop. "Twelve eggs, please," says Grandma. The woman gives thirteen eggs! "Is that twelve?" asks Tom. Tom counts the eggs. "Eleven, twelve, thirteen!" "One egg is for you!" says the woman. "Thank you!" says Grandma.
3
Eleven, twelve, thirteen!
Then they go to the bread shop. Grandma gives the bread to Tom. Tom holds it. "How many bananas have we got?" asks Grandma. "Five bananas!" says Tom. Pip looks at the bread. He wants the bread! Grandma and Tom smile. They have a good day at the market.
