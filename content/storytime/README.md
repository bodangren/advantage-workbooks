# Storytime Advantage content

One file per week of the kindergarten to P2 phonics program (track `storytime_week_package_20261022`).

- `l<level>/w<nn>.json`: the week package, schema `dashboard/lib/storytime/schema.ts` (`meta.program = "storytime"`, `meta.shape = "storytime-week"`). Level 1 = Anuban 2, Level 2 = Anuban 3, Level 3 = P1, Level 4 = P2.
- `l<level>/media/w<nn>/`: the pictures, the book and song audio, and the videos of the week.
- URL key `st<level>/<week>`; the public QR page is `/th/st/<level>/<week>`. The package keeps the path only (`home.songUrl`, `home.readAlongUrl`); the printed QR code adds the host `https://primary.reading-advantage.com` (the live Primary app, as the printed `/b/` codes; Monorepo, 2026-10-22). Daniel confirms the host before the first print run. Injector key: (program, level, week).
- Production rule exception (Storytime, 2026-10-22): a week before the first vowel (only Level 2 week 1, n) cannot have a decodable text. Its decodable is a sound book, `soundBook: true`: one picture word on each page ("A nest."), the tricky words of the week, and a names twist on the last page. Every later week keeps the 90 percent decodability rule. The `decodable` check (`check-weeks.ts --level <n>`) counts the title and the pages against the phonics graph v1 (`MASTERY_PHONICS_GRAPH`, or the mastery-advantage working tree): a word is decodable when the graph parse of the word is taught by the program week of the book (Level 2 week 1 = 33). Level 2 is CVC only (Storytime, 2026-10-22): two consonants before the first vowel (snap) need the initial-blend week, and two after the last vowel (naps) need the final-cluster week. The tricky words of Level 2 week 1 to the week also count. A name that the graph cannot decode by the week goes in the book's `names` list. Without the graph file, the check spells with the taught letters and says so in its label.
- Song: `song.lyricSheet` is the lyric exactly as the music model gets it (`[intro]`, `[verse]`, `[chorus]`, `[outro]` on their own lines); `song.lines` are its sung lines in order, each with a gesture and a picture. A generated song can sing other lines than the sheet (week 1: one sheet line is not sung, and the chorus repeats a line). Then `song.lines` and `song.times` follow the audio, and the `song-sheet` check gives a warning that names the first difference.
- English with Thai (`{ "en": ..., "th": ... }`): the step titles and notes, the prep lines, the song notes, the home card, and the three focus texts `focus.element.action`, `focus.paSkill`, and `focus.family.name`. An empty `th` prints the English.
- Period (Storytime, 2026-10-22): the 1-minute recalls of the steps moved to the next opening sit inside the 6-minute opening, so the opening routine is shorter on those days; the main steps fill the other 24 minutes. A seated step of 10 minutes has a stand-up break at minute 5.
- Sound game (Storytime, 2026-10-22): phonemic awareness is daily, in opening item 2 (1 minute; it replaces the calendar). `opening.pa` has one teacher line for each day: days 1 and 2 find the first and the last sound of the week, days 3 to 5 blend three sounds by ear.
- Family: 6 to `focus.family.max` picture words (8 by default, up to 10 for a family such as the numbers to ten).
- Friday check (E5): `circle-picture` rows for the sound, `circle-word` rows (the teacher says one printed word; the child circles it) for a decodable word and a tricky word, then `write-letter` and `write-word`. The answer of a circle row must be one of its choices.
- Friday check rows: a `circle-picture` row has `position` `first` (the default), `middle`, or `last`; a `write-letter` row can have the `sound` that the teacher dictates ("Write the letter for sss"). A `picture-sort` item has two or more columns (a sound and its pictures, the answer key); no picture is in two columns.
- Family words: each one is on a book page or in a song line (Storytime, week 5 review).
- Review week (`meta.kind = "review"`, design §3.5, Level 2 week 8): no books, no song, and no element. `block` gives the teaching weeks and the five days (the weeks of each day, a note, the two karaoke videos of the opening medley, which replace opening item 3, and `paper`: the review page of the day in the student book, with circle-word rows and a picture sort; Storytime, 2026-10-22). `benchmark` has the whole-class paper part (circle rows and dictated letters), the one-to-one sitting (the 26-letter strip in a mixed order, the taught letters on sound cards, the blend words, and the script with the four headings), and the reading record (the block decodables, copied with their text for the printed form). The teacher's book adds the benchmark page, a class record sheet of 30 rows, and one reading record form per decodable. The checks: days 1 to 4 cover every block week; the medley songs and the reading-record books (title and text) come from the block weeks; the strip has each letter once; the cards and the dictated letters are the taught letters. Minutes (Storytime, 2026-10-22): day 1 is paper 10, centers 10 with no rotation, close 4; one sitting is 3 minutes, so week 8 checks 27 children, and the others go to the week 9 Script E slot.
- Tags: a tag that an earlier week of the level taught goes in `reviewed`, not `taught`.
- Checks: `dashboard/lib/storytime/checks.ts`; `npx tsx scripts/storytime/check-weeks.ts --level <n>` (in `dashboard/`). Teacher's book PDF: `npx tsx scripts/storytime/make-manual-pdf.ts --level <n>` (in `dashboard/`, under the heavy-job lock).

The design is the Storytime track in the monorepo (`measure/tracks/storytime_advantage_program_20261021/design.md`).
