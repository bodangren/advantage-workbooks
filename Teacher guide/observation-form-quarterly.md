## DIMENSION 3: Classroom Technique
*Is the teacher running the room well?*
**Rate:** 1=Not Seen | 2=Developing | 3=Proficient | 4=Strong

### Tier 1 — Behavioral Foundation
- \_\_\_\_ **What to Do** — directions specific, concrete, sequential, observable
- \_\_\_\_ **Positive Framing** — names the wanted behavior, not the problem; group before individual
- \_\_\_\_ **Precise Praise** — praises the action not the child; no generic filler praise
- \_\_\_\_ **Least Invasive Intervention** — smallest correction that works; class not stopped for one student

**Positive-to-corrective ratio:** ☐ Below 1:1 | ☐ About 2:1 | ☐ 3:1 or better

### Tier 2 — Cold Call Safety
- \_\_\_\_ **Cold Call** — universal, predictable, neutral tone (never used as punishment)
- \_\_\_\_ **No Opt Out** — student who couldn't answer ended up saying the correct answer
- \_\_\_\_ **Wait Time** — 3+ seconds between question and name

### Tier 3 — Accuracy & Rigor
- \_\_\_\_ **Right Is Right** — no rounding up partial answers *(accuracy steps only)*
- \_\_\_\_ **Format Matters** — complete sentences expected and required
- \_\_\_\_ **Control the Game** — unpredictable reader changes, finger tracking *(Steps 3, 5)*

### The Answer Key Test
Right Is Right and No Opt Out apply **only** where a correct answer exists: Steps 2, 3, 5 (oral reading), 7, 9, 10C. They must **not** be applied to Steps 1, 4, 6, 10D, 11, 12, 13, where the student's choice *is* the answer.

- ☐ Teacher applied accuracy standards **only** in the accuracy domain
- ☐ **Critical:** Teacher did **not** correct student choices in Steps 4 or 6
