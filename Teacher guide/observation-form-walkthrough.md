## What to Look For (Based on Phase Observed)

### If Phase 1 (Steps 1-3): Orientation & First Reading
- ☐ **Step 1:** Students marked interest ratings
- ☐ **Step 2:** Vocabulary repeated chorally **and** individually
- ☐ **Step 3:** First reading uninterrupted — no vocabulary explanation during audio
- ☐ **Step 3:** Students marking the text (underline / ? / ! / ⭐)

**Student engagement level:** ☐ Low | ☐ Medium | ☐ High

---

### If Phase 2 (Steps 4-6): Controlled Re-engagement
- ☐ **Step 4:** Students chose their own words — 4 boxes, paragraph numbers filled in
- ☐ **Step 5:** Students wrote a main idea for each of the 3 paragraphs
- ☐ **Step 6:** Students chose their own sentences — 2 boxes, "why I chose it" ticked
- ☐ Teacher modeled thinking aloud ("I choose this because…")

**Student agency visible:** ☐ No | ☐ Somewhat | ☐ Yes

---

### If Phase 3 (Steps 7-8): Comprehension & Response
- ☐ **Step 7:** Think-pair-share used before answers were taken
- ☐ **Step 7:** Class answers entered — not the teacher's
- ☐ **Step 7:** Text evidence demanded for answers
- ☐ **Step 8:** Answer modeled collaboratively before individual writing

**Depth of discussion:** ☐ Superficial | ☐ Developing | ☐ Strong

---

### If Phase 4 (Steps 9-10): Practice
- ☐ Practice-focused — not lecture or grammar explanation
- ☐ Choral repetition used
- ☐ Brisk pacing maintained
- ☐ Workbook page completed after the activity
- ☐ *(Secondary only)* App activities run before the workbook page

**Practice quality:** ☐ Rote | ☐ Engaged | ☐ Purposeful

---

### If Phase 5 (Steps 11-13): Production & Reflection
- ☐ **Step 11:** Planning done before drafting
- ☐ **Step 11:** Quiet writing time protected
- ☐ **Step 12:** AI mediated — questions written before asking
- ☐ **Step 13:** Reflection completed before packing up

**Independence level:** ☐ Low | ☐ Medium | ☐ High
